Pintail Speed Ledger

Every banked benchmark run, release by release, against MySQL 8.4 and ClickHouse. The analytical suite runs 20,000,000 orders in containers capped at --cpus 8 --memory 8g; the oracle corpus runs 1,897 differential cases at scale 10,000. Each cell is a median and the multiple it represents.

Read the swings with care. Medians move ±10% between runs on a shared host, and some columns were measured on a busier machine than others — v0.0.4-rc1 is the clearest example, where every query reads three to four times its neighbours. When a median spikes but the per-query minimum holds, that is host noise, not a regression.

Oracle corpus — extended coverage

The differential corpus is a correctness suite, not a speed benchmark: 1,897 cases built to be semantically hostile. It measures what MySQL-exact semantics cost. The first block covers the 1,852 cases Pintail and MySQL both answer; the three-engine block covers the 1,227 all three answer.

ClickHouse is not answering the same suite. It errors on 634 of the 1,897 cases and times out on 21, and of the 1,242 it does answer, 274 disagree with MySQL. It is fully correct and participating on 953 — half the corpus. So its lead on shared ground is real engineering, but it is a lead over the half of the semantic surface it accepts, while Pintail answers 1,864 and matches MySQL on all but 60 unspecified-order cases.
